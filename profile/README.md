**Who this is for**

- Owners and CEOs who have paid for AI and want it doing real work.
- Department leads who want something their team can run this week.
- The person who does the work, who gets a precise way to prepare it and still makes the call.

**What these are for**

- **Your best way of doing it, for the whole team.** One piece of work, written down precisely enough that an assistant prepares it the same way every time.
- **Work you can check.** Every template says what it needs, what it produces, what it checked and what it could not.
- **A person decides.** Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for someone to approve it.
- **Tied into your business systems.** A workflow can be designed to pull from the systems your company already runs, and even push data back into them, with guardrails and a person approving. [Browse the Connectors Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/connectors/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=workflow-collections&utm_content=profile-readme&from=gh-profile-readme).
- **Free, open and portable.** MIT-licensed plain instructions that run in the assistant or agent your team already uses: ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, Codex, Copilot, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity.

**Quick start**

1. Pick a template for a piece of work your team does every week.
2. Paste it into your assistant and give it the files it asks for.
3. Read what comes back. It marks what it checked and what is left for you.
4. Stop there. You will know if this is for you. When you want it fitted to your company, or built into your agents, [that is what we do](https://fullypowered.ai/pricing/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=workflow-collections&utm_content=profile-readme&from=gh-profile-readme).
