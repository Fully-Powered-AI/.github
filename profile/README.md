# Fully Powered AI

Do the impossible with the team you have.

This is the home of the **Fully Powered Capability Templates**: 322 AI workflow templates for business teams, in 39 repositories by function and industry, free to read, copy and adapt under the MIT licence.

## What a capability template is

A capability is one piece of work your team does, such as preparing an overdue-invoice follow-up or a month-end close checklist. A capability template gives an AI assistant a precise way to prepare that work: the instructions it follows, the inputs it needs, the output it produces, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, and what has been checked.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## How to use one

Open a repository, then a folder. Each folder stands alone: start with its README, copy the folder you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini.

## What SKILL.md is

Each folder holds a file named `SKILL.md`. It is the technical file: the same instructions and output format as the template, in the packaged form that AI products which load instructions from a folder read. It is generated from `TEMPLATE.md`, so the two always agree. The capability template is the whole folder; `SKILL.md` is one way of loading it.

## What "checked" means

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and the record of one run on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Try a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
